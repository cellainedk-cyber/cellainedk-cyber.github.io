Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

IA-Net Studio uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API solely to authenticate authorized creator accounts and upload user-approved videos.

  • Data Collected: Basic TikTok profile identifiers (open_id, username, display name, avatar) and OAuth access/refresh tokens.
  • Storage & Sharing: Credentials are stored privately on the account owner's self-hosted server and are never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Revocation: Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
